## HR Handbook RAG Assistant: Hong Kong engineering firm, 2026

**Problem:** Staff need quick, reliable answers about leave, benefits and procedures. Those answers are in a long Chinese-language employee handbook.

**My role:** I designed and built the assistant end-to-end.

**Scope**
- Answers HR policy questions in Traditional Chinese, grounded in the handbook and citing its sources.
- Serves the relevant HR forms alongside each answer.
- Includes an employee chat and a separate admin console.

**Outcome:** a grounded self-service assistant for HR policy questions, checked against regression sets for answer accuracy, refusals and prompt injection.

**Stack:** Gemini (Vertex AI) · RAG · Python (FastAPI) · TypeScript, React · Google Cloud
